# Third-party material

The MIT licence in `LICENSE` covers the source code and shaders written for this project.
Third-party material keeps its own licence; see "Credits" in README.md. In
particular:

- Motion capture data (assets/character/soldier.qanim): derived from the 100STYLE
  dataset by Ian Mason, Sebastian Starke and Taku Komura, CC BY 4.0. Every clip in
  the file carries its source take, frame range and licence.
- Motion capture data, also in assets/character/soldier.qanim (the clips named cmu_*): the
  CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database, http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu. "The data used in this
  project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with funding from NSF
  EIA-0196217." Free to use and to include in products; not to be resold as data.
- ozz-animation (skeleton and clip runtime, fetched at build time): Guillaume Blanc, MIT.
- Action and death clips in assets/character/soldier.qanim (the clips not from 100STYLE or CMU):
  generated with Tencent HY-Motion 1.0 (text to motion) under the Tencent HY-Motion 1.0 Community
  License, which does not grant use in the EU, the UK and South Korea.
- Car engine sounds (assets/sounds/engines): rendered with engine-sim by Ange Yaghi, MIT.
- Sea wave function: after "Seascape" by Alexander Alekseev (TDM),
  CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 (non-commercial, share-alike).
- Character assets: MakeHuman / MPFB community assets (CC0, CC BY).
- Textures, sky and models under assets/: the licences of their sources.
- NVIDIA DLSS / Streamline and AMD FidelityFX binaries shipped with releases:
  the licences of NVIDIA and AMD.
